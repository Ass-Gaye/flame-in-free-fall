Research Impacts/Earth Benefits The flight data is being used to develop and validate sub-models of the flow within each flow regime. Understanding of the heat transfer mechanisms will enable space-based heat exchangers to be designed with much more confidence. Although this work is primarily aimed at thermal management subsystems and development of lightweight evaporators, it also impacts other systems for novel power and transmission, advanced chemical propulsion, and energy storage.
